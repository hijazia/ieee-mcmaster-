About Us

Supporting technical and professional growth at McMaster University

IEEE McMaster is dedicated to advancing technical excellence and leadership among McMaster students. As part of a global professional organization, we connect academic learning with practical experience through workshops, projects, and industry engagement.

Our activities are supported by a dedicated student lab located in Information Technology Building (ITB) 141, which serves as a collaborative space for technical development and chapter initiatives.

What We Do

IEEE McMaster organizes technical workshops, speaker events, networking sessions, and student-led projects. Our initiatives provide members with opportunities to build practical skills, explore emerging technologies, and engage with professionals from industry and academia.

All events and activities are open to students from all engineering and technical disciplines.

Why Join IEEE McMaster?

Membership in IEEE McMaster offers students the opportunity to develop hands-on technical and design skills, participate in workshops and chapter activities, take on leadership and executive roles, network with industry professionals and faculty, and access IEEE learning resources and publications. No prior experience is required to participate.

Our Chapters

Focused technical communities within IEEE McMaster

💻

Computer Chapter

IEEE Computer Society

The Computer Chapter explores modern computing technologies through both software and hardware perspectives. Activities include software development, embedded systems, microcontrollers, and introductory electronics, with an emphasis on practical implementation and technical fundamentals.

⚡

PES Chapter

Power and Energy Society

The PES Chapter focuses on power systems and energy technologies. Members explore topics such as power generation, electrical infrastructure, renewable energy, and sustainability through technical discussions and applied learning opportunities.

🏥

EMBS Chapter

Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society

The EMBS Chapter examines the intersection of engineering and healthcare. Activities cover biomedical technologies, medical devices, and health-focused engineering applications, emphasizing innovation and interdisciplinary collaboration.

Get Involved

Students can get involved by attending events, joining a chapter, or contributing to student-led initiatives. IEEE McMaster welcomes members from all backgrounds who are interested in learning and professional growth.
