Who We Are

About IEEE McMaster

Supporting technical and professional growth at McMaster University through hands-on learning and industry engagement.

Our Mission

IEEE McMaster is dedicated to advancing technical excellence and leadership among McMaster students. As part of the world's largest professional organization for engineering and technology, we connect academic learning with practical experience through workshops, projects, and industry engagement.

Our student-run lab in Information Technology Building (ITB) 141 serves as a collaborative space for technical development and chapter initiatives, providing members with hands-on access to tools and resources.

Technical Communities

Our Chapters

Focused technical communities within IEEE McMaster, each dedicated to advancing specific engineering disciplines.

💻

Computer Chapter

IEEE Computer Society

Explores modern computing technologies through both software and hardware perspectives. Activities include software development, embedded systems, microcontrollers, and introductory electronics, with an emphasis on practical implementation and technical fundamentals.

⚡

PES Chapter

Power and Energy Society

Focuses on power systems and energy technologies. Members explore topics such as power generation, electrical infrastructure, renewable energy, and sustainability through technical discussions and applied learning opportunities.

🏥

EMBS Chapter

Engineering in Medicine & Biology

Examines the intersection of engineering and healthcare. Activities cover biomedical technologies, medical devices, and health-focused engineering applications, emphasizing innovation and interdisciplinary collaboration.
